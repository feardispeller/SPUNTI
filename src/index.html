01The Weave

Raffia is pulled at dawn, while the fibre still holds the night's water. Each strand is rolled against the knee — the old way — until it sings under tension.

02The Dye

Indigo, cochineal and walnut hull, steeped in rain barrels behind the atelier. Colour arrives slowly here, the way dusk does.

03The Stitch

Waxed linen, a saddler's needle, and a rhythm you can hear from the courtyard. Every seam is finished blind, so the bag keeps its secret.
